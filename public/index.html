        About us

        The Student Clearance Document Request System is the official way to request school records from Cabiao Senior High School, without lining up at the Registrar’s Office.

        What this system is for

        Current students and alumni can request Form 137 / SF10, Good Moral Certificates, School Clearances and Report Cards online. Each request has a purpose: employment (local or abroad), university, military or other. Employment needs only your valid ID as evidence. For a university you give its name, and for the military you upload proof from the office or unit. The Registrar’s Office reviews it before release.

        Getting your document

        Every request gets a tracking number as soon as you submit it. Use it in Track my request to see if it is on-going or ready for pick-up, then claim your signed document at the Registrar’s Office.

        How a request moves

        1. You submit. Enter your LRN, choose a document and pick the purpose. For employment you upload your valid ID as evidence. For a university you give its name and upload proof, and for the military you upload proof from the office or unit. You get your tracking number right away.
        2. The Registrar reviews. The Registrar checks your uploaded documents, then approves the request or rejects it with a reason you can fix.
        3. You get notified. You verify your Gmail when you submit, and every update about your request (approved, rejected with the reason, ready for pick-up) is sent there. You can also follow it anytime with your tracking number. When it is ready, pick it up at the Registrar’s Office.
